# ⚡ Campus Energy Saving System

A smart and sustainable **Campus Energy Saving System** designed to monitor energy usage, identify wastage, and encourage efficient electricity consumption across a college campus.

This project combines **web development, Excel data analysis, Canva design, PowerPoint presentation, AI prompting, and mobile app prototyping** to create a practical solution for reducing unnecessary energy consumption.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Colleges use electricity for classrooms, laboratories, libraries, offices, hostels, and other facilities. Lights, fans, air conditioners, computers, and other electrical equipment may remain switched on when they are not required.

The **Campus Energy Saving System** aims to provide a digital solution that helps students and campus administrators understand energy consumption and take steps to reduce energy wastage.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- ⚡ Reduce unnecessary electricity consumption

## ✨ Key Features

### ⚡ Energy Monitoring
Track energy consumption across different areas of the campus.

Examples:
- Classrooms
- Laboratories
- Library
- Computer labs
- Offices
- Hostels
- Common areas

### 📊 Energy Data Analysis
Excel can be used to analyse sample energy-consumption data and identify:
- Daily energy usage
- Monthly consumption
- Area-wise consumption
- High-energy-consuming locations
- Energy-saving opportunities
- Consumption trends

### 🚨 Energy Wastage Detection
The system can highlight unusual or excessive energy consumption and help identify potential wastage.

### 💡 Energy-Saving Recommendations
The system can provide suggestions such as:
- Switch off lights when rooms are unoccupied
- Turn off fans and AC when not required
- Use energy-efficient lighting
- Reduce unnecessary equipment usage
- Encourage responsible energy usage

### 📱 Interactive App Prototype
A simple mobile application interface can display:
- ⚡ Current energy usage
- 📊 Energy statistics
- 🌱 Energy-saving progress
- 🚨 Wastage alerts
- 💡 Energy-saving tips
- 🏆 Campus energy-saving goals

### 🎨 Awareness Poster
A Canva-designed promotional poster can be used to encourage students and staff to participate in energy-saving activities.

### 🤖 AI Prompting
AI can assist with:
- Energy-saving ideas
- Data-analysis prompts
- Report generation
- Presentation content
- Awareness campaigns
- User-interface ideas
