# Campus Energy-Saving System -- Design Notes

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Campus Energy-Saving System

The Campus Energy-Saving System is a smart energy-management concept
designed to reduce unnecessary electricity consumption across
classrooms, laboratories, hostels, and common areas.

The design focuses on combining **energy-efficient devices, sensors,
automation, renewable energy, and energy monitoring** to create a
smarter and greener campus.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Poster Design Concept

The poster uses a **modern environmental and technology-focused visual
style**.

### Main Design Theme

-   Smart campus
-   Energy conservation
-   Renewable energy
-   Sustainability
-   Green technology
-   Automation

### Visual Style

-   Clean and professional layout
-   Large, bold headings
-   High contrast between text and background
-   Minimal decorative elements
-   Realistic renewable-energy background
-   Clear section-based information hierarchy

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Color Design

### Primary Color

**Dark/Navy Blue**

Used for the upper portion of the poster to communicate: - Technology -
Trust - Professionalism - Clean energy

### Secondary Visual Color

**Sky Blue**

Used in the transition toward the lower part of the poster to
represent: - Clean air - Open space - Sustainability - Renewable energy

### Natural Colors

The landscape uses green, brown, and natural tones to reinforce the
environmental theme.

### Text Color

**White**

White text is used throughout most of the design because it provides
strong contrast against the blue and landscape backgrounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Typography

The poster uses a bold sans-serif typography style.

### Main Heading

**CAMPUS ENERGY SAVING SYSTEM**

-   Large uppercase letters
-   Bold appearance
-   White color
-   Positioned at the top for immediate attention

### Supporting Tagline

**SAVE ENERGY • SAVE MONEY • SAVE OUR FUTURE**

The tagline is placed directly below the main heading to communicate the
project's purpose quickly.

### Section Headings

Examples: - SMART SOLUTIONS - BENEFITS - HOW IT WORKS - OUR GOAL

These headings are bold and larger than the body text, making the
information easy to scan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Layout Structure

The poster follows a top-to-bottom information hierarchy.

### Section 1 -- Project Title

Located at the top.

**Content:** \> CAMPUS ENERGY SAVING SYSTEM

**Purpose:** Introduces the project immediately.

### Section 2 -- Tagline

Located below the title.

**Content:** \> SAVE ENERGY • SAVE MONEY • SAVE OUR FUTURE

**Purpose:** Creates a short and memorable message.

### Section 3 -- Smart Solutions

Located in the upper-middle area.

**Features shown:** - LED lights - Motion sensor lighting - Automatic
fan/light control - Solar power - Smart energy monitoring

**Purpose:** Explains the main technologies used in the system.

### Section 4 -- Benefits

Located on the right side of the middle section.

**Benefits shown:** - Lower electricity bills - Reduced energy waste -
Lower carbon emissions - Smarter and greener campus

**Purpose:** Shows the practical impact of the proposed system.

### Section 5 -- How It Works

Located in the lower-middle section.

**Process described:** 1. Sensors detect occupancy and daylight. 2. The
controller switches appliances on or off automatically. 3. Energy usage
is monitored to identify waste.

**Purpose:** Gives a simple explanation of system operation.

### Section 6 -- Our Goal

Located near the bottom.

**Goal:** \> Reduce unnecessary electricity use across classrooms, labs,
hostels and common areas.

**Purpose:** Clearly communicates the project's final objective.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Visual Elements

### Wind Turbine

A large wind turbine is placed toward the lower-right portion of the
poster.

**Purpose:** - Represents renewable energy - Supports the sustainability
message - Adds visual interest - Connects the project to clean-energy
technology

### Landscape Background

The lower section contains a natural landscape with open fields and a
clear sky.

**Purpose:** - Reinforces the environmental theme - Creates a connection
between technology and nature - Makes the poster visually engaging

### Horizontal Divider

A thin line separates the main title from the tagline.

**Purpose:** Creates structure and improves visual hierarchy.

### Bullet Points

Circular bullet points are used for the Smart Solutions section.

**Purpose:** Makes multiple technical features easy to scan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Design Principles Used

### Visual Hierarchy

Important information is presented using larger and bolder text.

### Contrast

White text against dark blue and natural backgrounds improves
readability.

### Alignment

Content is arranged into clear sections instead of being randomly
distributed.

### Balance

The left-side Smart Solutions section is balanced by the Benefits
section on the right.

### Consistency

The same typography style and white text treatment are maintained across
sections.

### Simplicity

The design avoids excessive icons and decorative elements, keeping
attention on the project information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Key System Features

### LED Lighting

Replace conventional lighting with energy-efficient LED lights.

### Motion Sensors

Lights can respond to room occupancy and reduce unnecessary lighting
when areas are empty.

### Automatic Fan and Light Control

Automation can switch appliances on or off based on occupancy and usage
conditions.

### Solar Power

Solar energy can supplement campus electricity requirements using
renewable energy.

### Smart Energy Monitoring

Energy consumption can be tracked to identify high-usage areas and
possible energy waste.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Working Principle

The proposed system follows a simple automation cycle:

   ↓
